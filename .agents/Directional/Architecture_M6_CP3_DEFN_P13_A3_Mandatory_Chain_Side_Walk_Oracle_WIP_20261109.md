Otherwise reject the proposed region/exterior signature as unproven.

## Independent two-sided oracle (future product validator and TB)

For every original Mandatory arc `a`, independently build `occ[a] = [(region ID, ordinal in region.boundary, oriented occurrence)]` by **scanning every region boundary**, not by reading a candidate chain's claimed owners. Require unique incidence identity and A2b binding, and distinguish (i) two different region occurrences with opposite traversal directions, (ii) one proven physical boundary occurrence with a certified component-local exterior, from (iii) duplicated *physical-side* incidence, missing, or inconsistent incidence. Reject (iii) for joining even when the A2b baseline itself accepts the occurrence multiplicity. Two distinct occurrence ordinals may have the same region ID; until independent physical-side and ordered-walk proof exists, treat that same-region case as **uncertified**, not topologically impossible. Do not construct a region side from a sorted face key or count alone.

Given consecutive oriented candidates `a:u→v` and `b:v→w`, prove all of the following without using the producer's eligibility decision:

1. Both are **distinct original Mandatory arc IDs**, each tied through its network-edge ID to a checked member of the *same ordered* source rail; both source paths have nonzero exact pieces and meet at the same canonical `ExactSourcePoint` `v`. The concatenated ordered support has no gap/overlap or duplicated physical interval. Source-edge endpoints at parameter 0/1 canonically become `SourceVertexId` (`ExactSourcePath.h:46–54`).
2. The A2b node rotation at `v` has a single permissible incoming→outgoing continuation in its source-sheet fan. Refuse Trace/Cut contacts, branch, singularity port, protected corner, rail terminal or barrier. A synthetic A2b contact need not occur in `FieldAlignedCurveNetwork.nodes()`: do not assume missing network node means an ordinary rim vertex.
3. On the **forward/left** owned region walk, the occurrence of `b` must immediately follow `a` in cyclic boundary order with their own recorded orientations agreeing with the chain's direction. On the **reverse/right** walk, the occurrence of `a` must immediately follow `b`, since the region traverses `w→v→u`. For an exterior side, independently prove physical boundary status instead of inventing an exterior region occurrence. Two separate physical sides and occurrence ordinals must be certified; distinct region IDs are a sufficient but not necessary discriminator. Without separate same-region dual-side proof, classify the join as ineligible rather than treating the A2b input as invalid.
4. The ordered pair `(region/exterior, component, sheet)` must agree across both child arcs separately for each physical side. Use directed face-edge winding and exact source-face membership to derive sheet/component labels; **opposite** sides may carry different sheets. If chart transitions around the join cannot be shown orientation-consistent or unique, the join is ineligible. A source-face row index, position, digest, or arbitrary canonical winding cannot settle ambiguity.
5. Stop maximal-chain growth where either side fails; a completely uncut cycle is not an open unit and remains original singleton arcs pending a separate cycle proof. Normalize chain identity under full reversal plus **inversion of every child**, never an isolated child's sign flip.

The product is a partition: each original Mandatory arc appears once, preserving its original source supports and boundary occurrence indices. The verifier must reconstruct that bijection, the two side walks and rail order from original A2b/network/rail/source authority, **not** from an emitted aggregate count, producer cached owners, hash, or diagnostic-only snapshot. A producer-side legality check and its validator must not share an unchecked eligibility helper.

## Independent maximality proof (missing from earlier partition contract)

Construct a *certified endpoint-port join graph* from original A2b authority, not from the candidate output. The graph vertices are `(original NetworkArcId, First|Second endpoint)` slots derived from `GlobalTopologyArc::firstNode/secondNode`. Each original arc joins its two slots internally; each externally certified join connects exactly two slots of **different original Mandatory arcs**. Independently check the complete two-sided proof for each proposed external link in both travel directions. If more than one eligible external link claims **the same endpoint slot**, reject all competitors as `AmbiguousEndpointContinuation` instead of choosing a row- or ID-order winner. A route traverses one original arc **from one endpoint slot to its other slot** between external joins; an arc-ID-only graph can otherwise misclassify two links at the same end as a valid linear path. Enumerate maximal connected components by alternating original-arc traversals and certified external links. Exactly two free endpoint slots define a candidate open path; a component with no free slots is cyclic and remains singleton original arcs pending a cycle contract. Reject a candidate chain if its ordered exact source-support walk returns to its initial point or repeats any nonadjacent canonical source point/support interval on the same sheet (`NonSimpleChain`); a graph-open path is not automatically an embedded simple path. Canonicalize each surviving open path against its fully reversed, child-inverted sequence. The verifier must reconstruct this endpoint-slot graph independently, prove each emitted chain matches one entire eligible simple open path, that cyclic/ambiguous components stay separate, and no certified open-path link remains between emitted units; otherwise `NonMaximalPartition`.

A partition of only singletons is valid **only when no certified open-path link exists**. That distinguishes a genuinely unjoinable production source from a silent no-op implementation. Require a negative test that splits a two-child legal chain into singletons and fails specifically for nonmaximality; and a control where a Trace-contact, ambiguity or uncut cycle justifies singleton output. The TB production gate still requires a real Vase open-path component of at least two distinct original IDs; synthetic maximality evidence cannot replace it.
