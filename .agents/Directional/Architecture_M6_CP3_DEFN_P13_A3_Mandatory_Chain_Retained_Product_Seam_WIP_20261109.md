One rail can include multiple network edge IDs, and one network edge can yield several A2b Mandatory children after Trace splits.

The constructor and independent validator must also accept the *oriented* `meshWhole.F` (or an already-certified oriented-incidence product), bind it against source authority, and derive each side by the face's ordered directed edge, not by row number. `matches_source_faces()` checks sorted topology keys, **not winding**. Compare the ordered *side-specific* `(component, sheet, A2b region/exterior)` signature on each chain member. Distinct sheets on opposite physical sides do not by themselves forbid a join; mismatched lineage *along the same side* does.

## 2. Minimum candidate object and publication rule

The prospective retained geometry product is a partition of original `GlobalTopologyArcKind::Mandatory` IDs into ordered, oriented chains. Its semantic payload needs **only** ordered `GlobalTopologyOrientedArc` children per chain. No repeated copies of exact carriers, source-face rows, boundary occurrences, or rejected-join tables; derive each by checked lookups. Chain identity is its canonical original-arc sequence, orientation normalized by comparing the sequence against its fully reversed/inverted counterpart. Do not name code types, methods, or files after step `64B` or `D5`.

The join inventory is keyed by original-arc **endpoint slots** `(NetworkArcId, First|Second)`, derived from the existing A2b arc endpoints rather than stored in the published product. Each slot has at most one certified external link; following a chain must cross each member arc from one endpoint slot to the other. A degree-two arc-ID graph is insufficient when both links claim the same slot. Even a graph-open path is ineligible if the exact same-sheet source walk revisits a nonadjacent point or support interval, or returns to its start. The independent validator must reconstruct these cases and pre-register an endpoint-slot competition negative and a nonsimple-path negative. See the [side-walk proof](Architecture_M6_CP3_DEFN_P13_A3_Mandatory_Chain_Side_Walk_Oracle_WIP_20261109.md).

The constructor may publish only after independently testable validation establishes: (a) exactly-once coverage of every original Mandatory A2b arc; (b) source-rail ordered adjacency of consecutive children; (c) exact source-support endpoint continuity with no gap or overlap; (d) unique permissible rotation-system continuation excluding Trace/Cut contacts, protected corners, ports, hard barriers, rail terminals and branches; (e) exact agreement of two certified physical-side occurrences (their region IDs may coincide only with separate dual-side proof), or region/exterior, and direction on *both* sides; (f) matching component and side-specific sheet lineage; and (g) refusal to coalesce uncut cycles, true physical self-loops or uncertified same-region dual-side incidences. All unknown/ambiguous joins remain separate, typed as ineligible. A valid all-singleton partition has **not** demonstrated any chain reduction.

The independent verifier must reconstruct rail→source edge→network mandatory edge→A2b child mapping independently of the producer's join predicates and reject altered candidate children. Reusing `GlobalTopologyPlan::make_from_candidate()` as a precondition is sound, but sharing the producer's join decision helper or trusting a digest is not. Do not add a second independent rail graph, positional welding, floating tolerances, or a source-face row-order tiebreak.
