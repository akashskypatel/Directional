## Source-derived producer finding

In `src/geometry/PureQuadCompletion.cpp`, `complete_rectangular_grid` constructs a width × height grid from four corner provenance positions, choosing an interior target by corner-only bilinear interpolation. It does **not** interpolate the intermediate vertices of its four actual boundary sides. Each interior target is passed to `append_embedded_vertex` → `project_generated_point`, which uses `SurfaceProjectionBvh::project(target, allowedFaces)` and can select a closest allowed source face; no injective patch-coordinate mapping or cell-order certificate is consulted at that point. It then publishes all width × height quads and marks the backend `ClosedForm`. Only later does `completed_quads_have_simple_embedding` reject bad quads, and failure does not re-enter an alternative grid construction. The grid producer does not consume `completionVariant`, and `completion_variant_count` assigns `ClosedForm` exactly **one** variant; unlike bounded/pattern completion, the grid has no geometric or topological retry.

**Independent counterexample with a smooth-side continuation precondition, not a reproduction of Vase:** Construct a CCW 20-vertex planar boundary with the bottom side sampled at x=i/8, i=0..8, with rational heights [0,1/4,9/20,3/5,13/20,3/5,9/20,1/4,0]; right and left sides have two straight segments and the top has eight. This is a valid simple disk with side counts (8,2,8,2), area 19/32, and **every one of the seven consecutive bottom-segment pairs meets the source's 45-degree same-logical-side alignment threshold** (minimum cosine 0.72414 > 1/sqrt(2)). With the same-family/source-identity prerequisites, the bottom need not split into extra logical sides. For this width=8,height=2 grid, corner-only bilinear interpolation places all middle-row points at y=1/2, including center (1/2,1/2), outside the patch. Independent polygon predicates find 14/16 valid and 8/16 patch-contained quads. Side-aware Coons placement at y=(bottom(u)+1)/2 produces **16/16 valid and contained quads, exact union equal to the patch**, center (1/2,33/40). This is an existence counterexample to the *algorithm's* corner-only premise, not proof that every Vase descriptor is repairable with Coons or that the exact source provenance/field prerequisites hold on Vase. The earlier 8-vertex toy example has an excessively sharp bottom turn and is **not a demonstrated production-reachable descriptor**; do not use it to claim reachability.

**Decisive P13-h correction — patch 4 is a boundary-only strip:** `complete_rectangular_grid` initializes `nextInterior=-1` and generates negative interior vertex IDs. Its first quad is `grid[0][0],grid[0][1],grid[1][1],grid[1][0]`; when width and height both exceed 1, `grid[1][1]` is negative. The TB69 first quad `5349,5350,8629,8628` contains four **positive** original `mesh.quads` IDs; therefore **width=1 or height=1**, with **no generated interior vertices anywhere in that grid**. Interior bilinear interpolation and source projection cannot cause the first Vase failure. Earlier language suggesting that either caused the first fold was incorrect and is superseded here. Patch 4 instead needs boundary ordering, strip pairing, and source-boundary embedding investigation. Original `vase.off` faces 155 and 248 share edge 255–1248, so the logged first locus does not support a distant-sheet merge. This deduction says nothing about the other 223 rectangular failures; the independent Coons witness still demonstrates a *separate* interior construction defect.
