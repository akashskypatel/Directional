## Correct architectural distinction

`sourceFaceSheets` is a **local isolation authority**, not by itself source-topological boundary authority.

Maintain two distinct concepts:

### Source-topological producer region

A producer region is assembled through exact source-mesh adjacency. It is split only by authoritative topology/barrier conditions whose semantics require a producer boundary, including genuine source boundaries and hard-feature/barrier edges. Region construction may not use Euclidean proximity, nearest-point capture, raw IDs, discovery order, counts, frequency, or fixture-specific conditions.

A non-hard shared source edge does not become an exterior region boundary solely because its incident faces carry different local isolation-sheet labels.

### Local isolation sheet

The existing local sheet labels remain mandatory for operations where nearby unrelated geometry must not interact accidentally: proximity capture, projection, collision/candidate association, nearest-sheet decisions, and other geometric locality checks already protected by G2.

Cross-sheet transport is allowed only through the exact shared source edge in the source topology and only when reciprocal field transition metadata is valid. This does not authorize Euclidean joining of sheets or capture across space.

Both topology-region and isolation-sheet provenance must remain observable. Do not silently overwrite or reinterpret the existing local sheet label as the new topology-region ID.
