## Answer (b) — UNREACHABLE through the supported checked-construction path

A `SourceTopologyRegions` containing three source triangles on the same hard-feature edge **can** be assembled in isolation: `SourceTopologyRegions::make` (`src/geometry/SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:7823-7907`) checks unique face topology, complete face/member bindings and typed region/component/sheet consistency, but does not enforce the global undirected source-edge valence. **This fact alone does not reach the targeted factory branch.** The factory has a mandatory independent, exact-source-matching A3 atlas gate *before* the incidence census. Under the checked `TriMesh::set_mesh`/`FieldTransportAtlas::make` authority contract, such an atlas cannot be constructed for three incident source triangles.

### Complete deciding guard chain

1. **G1 — face-derived mesh topology.** `TriMesh::set_mesh` (`include/directional/core/TriMesh.h:331-351`) calls `compute_edge_quantities` unconditionally even when `_EV/_FE/_EF` are supplied; supplied tables must be bit-identical to derived tables or an exception is thrown. The prior RA-48.3 injected-table escape was closed; see frozen RA-51.6 and RA-54.6. `compute_edge_quantities` (`:156-164`) calls `dcel.init(V,F)` and then `dcel.check_consistency(verbose, true, true, true)`, throwing on failure.
2. **G2 — strict directed-edge uniqueness.** `DCEL::check_consistency` section 5 (`include/directional/core/DCEL.h:1453-1492`) rejects any duplicate directed source halfedge when `checkHalfedgeRepetition=true`. Three triangles sharing an undirected edge necessarily supply three oriented appearances in only two possible directions; at least two directed halfedges therefore coincide. The check rejects the mesh before a checked atlas can be constructed, independent of triangle orientation.
3. **G3 — no alternate atlas construction.** `FieldTransportAtlas::make` is the public construction seam (`include/directional/authority/FieldTransportAtlas.h:827-841`), takes a `TriMesh` and a `SourceTopologyRegions`, and requires that `SourceTopologyRegions::matches_source_faces(mesh.F, mesh.V.rows())` (`src/authority/FieldTransportAtlas.cpp:1893-1910`). Atlas construction and stored authority are private/immutable (`FieldTransportAtlas.h:899-954`); no supported alternate field/edge-table constructor bypasses G1/G2.
4. **G4 — exact factory pin is the deciding conjunct.** `SurfacePhaseFrontProduct::make` (`src/geometry/SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:8115-8144`) rejects if the atlas is null, the regions do not match the provided face matrix, **or `fieldTransportAtlas->matches_source_faces(sourceFaces, sourceTopologyRegions, sourceVertexCount)` is false**. The latter method (`src/authority/FieldTransportAtlas.cpp:3090-3110`) checks vertex count, per-row face topology, region and component against immutable atlas records. A valid atlas from a different manifold mesh cannot authenticate the three-incidence input; with no valid same-source atlas the factory returns `AtlasSourceMismatch` before `railIncidenceCounts` is considered.
5. **G5 — separately, the ordinary produced-network path rejects first.** `build_surface_cell_network` recounts all source triangles per hard-feature edge and rejects `sourceIncidences > 2` at `src/geometry/SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:18820-18857`. This is **not** the decisive factory-boundary proof because the factory is independently callable; G1-G4 are.

The optional nonempty `sourceFaceBranchRotations` incidence check (`SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:8184-8213`) can also reject a three-face edge before the target branch, but is **not** part of the general proof: that vector may be empty. Similarly, the mere existence of a typed overfull error code is not evidence of a live disjunct.

Do not delete or weaken the guard at `SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:8240-8277`: its count-based rejection is fail-closed defense if an authority construction or mesh-mutation seam changes later.
