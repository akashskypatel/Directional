## B. Two independent proofs, not one producer-derived receipt

**Product-level proof** (on the already validated A2b input products, not on the candidate's claims): enumerate every `GlobalTopologyArcKind::Mandatory` original `NetworkArcId`; recover each `mandatoryEdge` by explicit `NetworkEdgeId` lookup in `FieldAlignedCurveNetwork::mandatory_edges()` (`find_mandatory_edge` is keyed by **source-edge topology**, not network-edge ID). Recover original exact `sourcePath`, endpoint IDs and directed parameters; index `SurfaceCellRail::sourceEdges/sourceVertices` in their own certified order; derive oriented adjacent faces from `sourceMesh.F`, cross-check their sorted keys against `SourceTopologyRegions` **only to obtain source-face/component/sheet authority**, and independently bind each *directed A2b arc side* to its specific graph-face orbit by re-deriving the published `GlobalTopologyPlan` rotation and oriented region-boundary occurrences (`GlobalTopologyPlan.cpp:1109–1188,1215–1281`). One source-face topology key can support multiple embedded fragments/orbits, so source-region membership, sheet ID and face-row identity do **not** certify which original A2b region owns that side. Reconstruct **original A2b region boundary occurrences**, with ordered region, ordinal and sign; match each directed side to exactly one correct occurrence and its oriented source face, including a physical exterior only after verifying exactly one incident source face and the corresponding exterior orbit. If the retained public inputs do not support this independent binding, fail closed and return to Review instead of trusting a producer-local orbit label. Check each emitted child is one original arc, each original Mandatory arc appears exactly once, no Trace/Cut arc appears, and each chain's exact carriers concatenate without gap, overlap, inversion or changed endpoints.

**Independent join/maximality proof** (do not invoke the builder's join helper, rejected-join flags or hash): enumerate candidate transitions from consecutive source edges on the **same** `HardRailId`, then check distinct `NetworkEdgeId` ancestry, compatible region/exterior side-walk continuity on both sides, compatible same-side component/sheet, unique compatible A2b rotation and no Trace/Cut incidence, port, branch, **corner/junction independently derived from source V and feature-map edges/options**, rail terminal, or HardFeature/Boundary kind conflict. Reject unknown predicate inputs rather than guessing. Construct the independent eligible-join graph: each open-path component must equal exactly one fully populated emitted chain, up to complete reversal with inversion of every child; uncertified closed components cannot be merged. If two output units leave an independently eligible link between them, reject for **nonmaximal partition**. An all-singleton partition cannot pass a positive reduction test when an eligible open-path link exists.

**Non-vacuity on the production Vase execution:** at least one emitted chain must contain `>=2` distinct original A2b Mandatory IDs **and** `>=2` distinct `NetworkEdgeId` ancestors, with independently reconstructed exact support, two-sided region/exterior occurrences, legal rotation and source sheet/component lineage. A pair of Trace-split siblings from one network edge, one arc represented by two support pieces, aggregate counts (145 Mandatory or 164 exterior subdivisions), or a synthetic positive alone is insufficient.
