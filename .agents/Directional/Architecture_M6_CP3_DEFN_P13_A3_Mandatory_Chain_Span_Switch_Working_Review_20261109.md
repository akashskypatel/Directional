## Independently inspected coupling requiring resolution before freezing patches

1. **Span cardinality and region ends:** `src/geometry/GlobalConformityBaseline.cpp:287` and `GlobalConformityBaselineValidator.cpp:354` require one span per `topology.arcs()`. `GlobalConformityBaseline.cpp:345–360` and Validator around line 390 derive the boundary span from each child `NetworkArcId` and append region ends per child incidence. Merely replacing the count by the number of chains leaves this mapping inconsistent, and multiple child arcs from one chain can incorrectly create more than two ends for one span. A canonical arc→chain-span map, a preserved child-occurrence identity, and region-side multiplicity rules must be specified together. A child-arc list must not silently become a list of distinct region ends; parity and coverage require deliberate semantics.

2. **Curved-disk path and breakpoint positions:** `src/geometry/SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:17642` rejects any breakpoint with `supportPieceIndex != 0`, and line 18433 requires schedules sized to A2b arc count. Several source-arc/boundary sites convert `NetworkArcId` directly into `ConformitySpanId`. A multi-piece Mandatory chain cannot be consumed by simply changing a span lookup; each child must receive its **exact oriented piece offsets and point restriction** from one chain schedule. Both directions, shared/reciprocal boundaries, source-chart and feature stops must be preserved. CB112 must be behaviour-neutral with one-piece spans, but its implementation design must handle later arbitrary piece offsets.

3. **Global ordinal-unit assumptions:** `SurfaceCellTracing.cpp:9386–9397` and `:19087–19095` require each shared interval to have ordinal width exactly 1; `:9679–9730` assumes unit coverage. But a chain of three source edges can be assigned a *single* positive A3 count (the CB110 support receipt demonstrates count 1 versus three per-arc counts 1). One chain-level ordinal unit must then span multiple source pieces. Per-piece fractional exact intervals or an equivalently certified chain-owned interval representation is necessary, and all reciprocal-front, coverage, boundary and trace consumers must agree. A patch changing only `supportPieceIndex` is insufficient. Do not weaken the unit-coverage checks into unconditional acceptance.

4. **Stops belong upstream:** RA-218 §2 supplies the authority: feature-map `corners`/`junctions` and singularity ports, plus degree/rail identity from A2b. The CB110 `derive_mandatory_chain_partition` and `derive_mandatory_chain_support` already exist in `GlobalConformityBaselineSupport.{h,cpp}`. CB111 should add a pure, unwired typed stop derivation and reject inconsistent source-vertex/port/node references. It must not reimplement the floating-angle feature-corner predicate or create a diagnostic sidecar.
