## 3. Two conceptually possible repair shapes, producer gates and falsifiers

### A. Publisher-side cut at an authenticated rail junction

**Shape:** A4 would publish carrier-local front spans whose endpoint attachments and `CanonicalRoute` each belong to the same source carrier (and reconstitute exact paired halves/cell-side incidence). Its new junction boundary, if any, must come from the accepted A2b source/rail schedule with source-vertex/domain identity and A3 adjacency; not from an epsilon-near vertex or inferred angular fan. It must preserve the entire original route, `sharedBoundaryInterval` ownership/ordinals, opposite-edge pairing, closed cells, region/sheet barriers, and protected hard rails. A front-side split cannot create a zero-length orphan, counterfeit a second carrier, or independently move one side of a paired boundary.

**What is already produced:** `SurfaceFrontEdge`, `SurfaceTracePoint`, `SurfaceSharedBoundaryInterval`, `CanonicalRoute`, and `SurfaceHardRailFieldTransition`, at the header/producer loci in §2. **Missing:** a published exact *junction split occurrence* tied to the source vertex, the front-side parametric location, source carrier side, and A2b schedule, plus a producer that remints **both** matching front edges and affected cell boundary paths consistently. The current edge publisher changes `observedSteps` only at source-edge topology changes (`:12573-12579`, `:12641-12659`) and does not split already-built front/cell geometry. **As an implementation using only existing witnesses, A is NOT YET ADMISSIBLE.** A new producer and an upstream proof are prerequisites; this document does not authorize either.

**Concrete falsifier:** If the materialized front's full source-supported boundary path contains no nondegenerate occurrence of the junction at which it can be partitioned into carrier-homogeneous intervals with consistent paired-cell ownership, splitting cannot repair this case without inventing geometry. The measured 21 junction identities are the separating population: archive TSV indices **12–25, 34–36, 39, 40, 42, 43**, specifically historical ordinals **115–117, 122, 130, 132, 134, 137, 139–143, 150, 404, 406, 407**, plus four supplemental identities. In all 21 raw logs `routeLength=1`; a mechanical split of that already-published *single-carrier* route at its endpoint cannot manufacture a valid second carrier. This **already falsifies the naive split-only variant**, but does not falsify a redesigned producer that supplies an independently verified additional segment. The 23 interior-shaped cases are control cases that must not regress.

### B. Consumer-side source-vertex-aware admission

**Shape:** The consumer would accept a nonincident endpoint face pair only under a **producer-issued**, source-domain-labeled, unique and rail-local star certificate proving the common source vertex, the two hard-edge carrier incidences, the two nonhard sector walks, the reciprocal A3 transitions and their composed orientation, exact endpoint attachment support, and consistency with the selected A4 route and rail/region/sheet. A rejected or ambiguous cycle must stay rejected. A vertex match or a derived chart traversal computed opportunistically during acceptance is not sufficient authority.

**What is already produced:** edge-keyed `SurfaceHardRailFieldTransition`, `SurfaceTracePoint`, `SurfaceFrontEdge`, `SurfaceHardRailTerminalContacts` and the **post-admission** `SurfaceHardRailRouteJunctionCertificate` in §2. **Missing:** an upstream vertex-keyed A2b/A3 **junction-sector certificate** with a named producer, type, and publication site; neither `SurfaceHardRailTerminalContacts` nor the existing post-admission junction record supplies it. **B is NOT YET ADMISSIBLE** under RA-65.5; Gate C remains SUSPENDED. A proposal that merely loosens `oriented_carrier` to accept a common vertex would remove the source-edge barrier without substituting an equivalent proof and is not a repair.

**Concrete falsifier:** For any claimed positive vertex-star certificate, enumerate the complete source-link cyclic face set, both hard spokes and nonhard arcs, and compare the A3 composed quarter-turn along each route. A second distinct admissible sector path, a missing/nonmanifold spoke, conflicting A3 transport, wrong rail domain, or an attachment not owned by the attested sector *retires that certificate* rather than permitting fallback. Use the same **21 junction identities** (15 un-compacted / six compacted) as potential positive witnesses and the **23 interior-shaped indices 1–11, 26–33, 37–38, 41, 44** as negative controls. The 21 demonstrate common-vertex shape, **not** a published sector witness. The index domains must be labeled separately when comparing results.
