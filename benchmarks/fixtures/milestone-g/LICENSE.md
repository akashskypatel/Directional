# Milestone G fixture licensing

The analytic meshes and cross fields stored in this directory are deterministic project-authored fixtures generated by `generate_milestone_g_fixtures.py` and may be used under the same license as Directional.

`bunny_1k_random.obj` and `vase.off` are externally supplied production regression artifacts and are intentionally not generated or modified by the fixture script. Their benchmarks intentionally omit prescribed fields so Directional must calculate the cross field. Redistribution must follow the provenance and license terms recorded by the repository maintainer; this fixture record does not assert that either mesh is project-authored.
