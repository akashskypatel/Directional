# Milestone G fixture licensing

The analytic meshes and cross fields stored in this directory are deterministic project-authored fixtures generated by `generate_milestone_g_fixtures.py` and may be used under the same license as Directional.

`bunny_1k_random.obj` is the externally supplied random-triangulation bunny regression artifact and is intentionally not generated or modified by the fixture script. Its benchmark intentionally omits a prescribed field so Directional must calculate the cross field. Redistribution of the bunny asset must follow the provenance and license terms recorded by the repository maintainer; this fixture record does not assert that the mesh is project-authored.
